From the incident investigation and corrective action packages in ashley-eyekyam/hse-leadership-skills (Apache-2.0) it takes: replacing the names of the people involved with labels before any analysis and keeping the key apart; a numbered evidence log and a timeline in time order before any cause is named; tying every cause to evidence, so an unsupported cause is never a root cause; keeping immediate, contributing and root causes apart and pressing past a person's act to a cause the organisation controls; checking whether that equipment or task has been involved in earlier incidents; ranking actions by the order of controls, with an owner, a date and a link to a cause on each; for every cause, one action that puts this occurrence right and at least one that keeps it from happening again; a dated check on every action, with no action counted as working before its check result is written down; a check that looks for the intended effect rather than for finished work; a stopgap control while a permanent fix is pending; and caution before a record is shared widely. Fully Powered AI added: one plain cause method in three layers; stated column headers for the evidence and action lists, with a stop when one does not match; owners, dates and checks taken only from people, with any action the assistant adds marked as suggested and not agreed; the register carried across update runs to verified closure, with its statuses and flags; reporting duties read only from the company's own written rules and confirmed by its qualified person, never decided; numbered questions for the investigation lead; the stop when police, a regulator, an insurer or a claim is involved; and the rule that the assistant blames no one, reports nothing and sends nothing.
