# Pilot acceptance-test preparation

Go into every AI pilot with the test already written: the cases that matter, what good looks like for each and the rule your owner will use to decide.

*Workflow template · Operations and process improvement · version 0.1.0 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflow templates/pilot-acceptance-test-preparation/)*

## The outcome

Every pilot ends in a clear decision, backed by evidence the whole team can read. Before the workflow touches real work, the pilot owner holds a complete acceptance test, written to your best person’s standard. A pilot that passes becomes a workflow the whole team relies on from its first week.

## Who starts the work, and when

The pilot owner starts it: the operations lead, department head or firm administrator accountable for the workflow’s result. They start once the purpose is agreed, before anyone has seen an output. The expert, usually the person who does the work best today, supplies the hard cases.

The AI assistant prepares the test pack. The owner and the expert run every test, mark every result and decide.

## What they supply

- **The workflow’s purpose.** What it prepares, for whom, and where its job ends.
- **Its inputs and output.** The records it reads and the output a person reviews.
- **A few past examples the team trusts.** Finished work the expert would sign, with the inputs behind it.
- **The known awkward cases.** The files, accounts or requests that trip people up, one sentence each.

## What comes out

An acceptance test pack: cases with their expected behavior, a rubric, a record sheet and a pass rule.

A good pack lets a reviewer who has never seen the workflow mark any run.

See the worked example with invented data in [EXAMPLE.md](EXAMPLE.md).

## What has to change for your company

Your owner names the expert and sets the local rules: which record wins when two disagree, who receives each kind of question, how many runs each case gets and the pass rule. The test runs in the AI workspace your team already uses, on ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini, with records your business permits there.

## Start using it

1. Read [EXAMPLE.md](EXAMPLE.md) to see what a good result looks like.
2. Open [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md) and set every item under "What to customize" to your own rules, roles and limits.
3. Give your AI assistant the instruction block and the output format from the template, with your own limits in place of the ones the instructions leave open in square brackets. Paste them in as custom instructions in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini, or copy this whole folder into a product that loads `SKILL.md` packages. This folder stands alone; you do not need the rest of the repository.
4. Run it on past work first, using the reviewer checklist and acceptance tests in the template, before anyone relies on it.
