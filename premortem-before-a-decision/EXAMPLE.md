# Premortem before a decision: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* The company, its people, its customers and every date and figure below are invented. The company is an invented 40-person distributor of cleaning supplies, deciding whether to sign a seven-year lease on a larger warehouse and move into it over one weekend. People are named by role only. First come the brief and the written reasons the facilitator typed up, then the grouping sheet from the first pass, then the room's ratings and notes, and last the premortem record from the second pass.

## The inputs to the first pass

### 1. The brief (written by the owner)

```
Decision: Whether to sign a seven-year lease on a larger warehouse 12 miles
  from the current one, and move into it over one weekend.
Plan: Sign the lease by Friday 13 November 2026. Racking installed in
  January. Move over the weekend of 6-7 February 2027. The current
  building's lease ends on 31 March 2027.
Hard to reverse because: seven-year lease; a deposit of three months' rent;
  the current building will be let to someone else once we give notice.
Decides: Owner
Go decision due: Friday 13 November 2026
Imagined failure date: 31 May 2027
Participants: P1 Owner; P2 Operations manager; P3 Warehouse lead;
  P4 Customer service lead; P5 Finance lead; P6 Senior driver
Facilitator: Office manager (no part in the move decision)
Session: Tuesday 3 November 2026
Reasons written privately before discussion: yes
Settings: defaults
```

### 2. The written reasons (typed by the facilitator, as written)

Here the reasons are typed sheet by sheet so the example is easy to follow. In a real session the facilitator types them in a mixed order, because the R numbers follow the typing order and would otherwise show who wrote what.

```

P1,"The move weekend ran over and we could not ship on the Monday or Tuesday; two of our biggest customers ordered elsewhere."
P1,The rent on the new building plus the move cost more than planned and cash got tight by April.
P1,Customers near the old site got later deliveries and complained.
P2,The racking was not installed by the move date because the installer took longer than quoted.
P2,"Stock counts were wrong after the move, so we sold things we did not have."
P2,We moved in the same week as a big seasonal order and could not cope.
P3,Two of the four pickers left because the new site is 12 miles further out and there is no bus.
P3,The loading dock only takes one truck at a time and incoming deliveries queued.
P3,Pallets were labelled badly during packing so nobody could find anything for a week.
P4,We did not tell customers early enough and they were surprised by delays.
P4,Phones and the internet line were not working on the Monday at the new site.

P5,"The fit-out cost more than budgeted (power for the forklift chargers, the office)."

```

### 4. The facilitator's notes on checks

```
Today: Wednesday 4 November 2026

G1 Orders not shipped after the move weekend
- Check: get the moving company's written hour-by-hour plan for the move
  weekend. Owner: Operations manager. Due: Tuesday 10 November 2026.
  Status: not done.
- Warning sign: share of pallets racked at the new site during the move
  weekend. Act when: under half by 6 pm on Saturday 13 February; the owner
  then decides whether Monday's orders ship from the old building.
  Watched by: Operations manager.

G2 Racking, dock and yard not ready
- Check: racking installer to confirm in writing an installation start at
  least two weeks before the move. Owner: Warehouse lead. Due: Friday
  6 November 2026. Status: done.
- Check: walk the new yard with the largest truck to confirm it can turn.
  Owner: Senior driver. Due: Monday 16 November 2026 (on routes until
  then). Status: not done.
- Warning sign: installer has not started work at the new site. Act when:
  no start by Monday 25 January 2027; the owner then reviews the move date.
  Watched by: Warehouse lead.

G6 Pickers leave over the longer commute
- Check: map travel options to the new site for each shift (bus, car-share,
  parking) and talk them through with the pickers. Owner: Warehouse lead.
  Due: Wednesday 11 November 2026. Status: not done.
- Warning sign: pickers giving notice or asking to change shifts once the
  move is announced. Nobody agreed what number means act.
  Watched by: Warehouse lead.

G8 Phones and internet not working on the first day
- Everyone agreed: order the new internet line and the move of the phone
  numbers at least 60 days before the move. Nobody took it. The finance
  lead thinks the line order takes 30 to 60 days. No warning sign agreed.

Changes to the plan agreed in the room:
- Move weekend changed from 6-7 February to 13-14 February 2027, after the
  spring order for Customer A ships on 10 February.
- Keep the old building's dock in use until Wednesday 17 February 2027 as a
  fallback for shipping.

Next premortem: Friday 15 January 2027. Sooner if the move date changes or
the racking installer's start date moves.
```
