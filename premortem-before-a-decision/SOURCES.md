# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. The method is the project premortem, published as an open-source package in deciqAI/knowledge-skills under the MIT licence. From its main instructions: picturing the plan as having already failed and explaining why, each person writing alone before any discussion, grouping and rating the causes, giving the serious ones an owner and a sign to watch, and setting the date of the next premortem. From its worked example of a start-up whose plan failed: a facilitator with nothing riding on the answer, one participant from outside the plan, and named events that bring the next premortem forward. Fully Powered AI added the two passes around the room's own ratings, a record that never says who wrote which reason, the count of how many people named each cause, the fixed ranking with its tie order, the rule that a serious harm with no warning is always a top risk, the flag on a serious cause only one person named, checks counted against the go date, every gap turned into a question for the owner, and a decision line the record leaves blank. No third-party text is reused.
