# SOP exception capture: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* An invented regional building-supplies distributor: the procedure, then one step of its register and the open questions.

**New-customer account setup**

Sales operations · Version 2

1.  Receive the signed account application from the account executive.
2.  Check the business details against the application.
3.  Send the application to the credit controller for terms.
4.  Collect the tax exemption certificate where one applies.
5.  Create the account in the order system.
6.  Send the welcome pack and first-order instructions.

**Owner:** Operations lead

**Exception register · New-customer account setup · Step 4, collect the tax exemption certificate**

- **Missing decisions:** What to do when the certificate is expired, issued to another entity or promised to follow.
- **Stop conditions:** The certificate name differs from the account’s legal name. The certificate has expired.
- **Who is asked:** The finance lead.
- **Expert’s rule:** Set the account up as taxable until a valid certificate in the account’s legal name is on file. Never mark an account exempt on a promise.
- **Evidence:** Account 5518: marked exempt on a note; the certificate arrived in the parent company’s name. Coordinator: “I set them up taxable and switch them when the certificate is in.”

**Open questions for the operations lead**

- **Question 1:** With no trade reference, one coordinator holds the account; another opens it on cash-on-delivery terms. Which is the rule? Evidence: Accounts 5509 and 5530.
- **Question 2:** Who releases a first order before setup is complete? The account executive and the credit controller each name themselves.

Status: draft for the operations lead’s approval. The procedure has not been changed.
