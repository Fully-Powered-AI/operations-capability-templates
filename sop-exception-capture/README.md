# SOP exception capture

Turn what your most experienced person does with the awkward cases into an exception register for one procedure. Once the owner approves it, every colleague handles those cases the way your best person does.

*Workflow template · Operations and process improvement · version 0.1.0 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflow templates/sop-exception-capture/)*

## The outcome

Every step of the procedure carries the decisions, stop conditions and escalation paths your best people already use, each traced to a real case. A new hire handles the awkward case in their first week. An AI assistant preparing the work flags it and names whom to ask. The whole team works at the level of its best person, and that is how you 10X what it can do.

The people who do the work are the source of every rule. Where colleagues disagree, the owner settles it once.

## Who starts the work, and when

The procedure’s owner starts it: an operations lead, a controller, a firm administrator or a people lead. Start before a procedure goes to AI or a new hire, after the same mistake happens twice, or before the one person who knows the exceptions changes role.

## What they supply

- The written procedure, or a transcribed walkthrough where there is none.
- Three to five recent real cases, including one that went sideways, with client details removed.
- Thirty minutes with the person who does the work.

## What comes out

You get an exception register for the procedure. For each step it records the missing decisions, the stop conditions, who is asked, the rule the expert follows and the evidence (which case, which words). Open questions where colleagues disagree come last, for the owner to settle. Nothing changes in the procedure until the owner approves.

A good register uses the expert’s words, cites a case for every rule and leaves each disagreement for the owner.

## What has to change for your company

Name the owner who settles disagreements. Decide which cases can be shared and what client detail comes out first. Set your own thresholds, and agree where the approved register lives and who approves later changes. The register then becomes the job aid and the instructions an AI assistant works from, on ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini. Your organization keeps control of how the work gets done.

## Start using it

1. Read [EXAMPLE.md](EXAMPLE.md) to see what a good result looks like.
2. Open [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md) and set every item under "What to customize" to your own rules, roles and limits.
3. Give your AI assistant the instruction block and the output format from the template, with your own limits in place of the ones the instructions leave open in square brackets. Paste them in as custom instructions in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini, or copy this whole folder into a product that loads `SKILL.md` packages. This folder stands alone; you do not need the rest of the repository.
4. Run it on past work first, using the reviewer checklist and acceptance tests in the template, before anyone relies on it.
