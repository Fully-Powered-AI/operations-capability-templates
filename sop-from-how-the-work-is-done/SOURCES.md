# Sources and credits

Written by Fully Powered AI in its own words. Two MIT-licensed public packages informed the method: microsoft/cat-agent-skills (settle where a procedure starts and ends first, give every step one owner and every decision both paths, mark what is missing, invent no limits, split a long procedure) and alirezarezvani/claude-skills (a named owner and review date, a visible check for each step, more than the trouble-free path). No third-party text is reused.

- **Fully Powered AI:** Its own working guides, and its published companion, SOP exception capture.
- **[microsoft/cat-agent-skills](https://github.com/microsoft/cat-agent-skills)** at commit `3158d47b2ae78e98027487d7a744827931a20d0a`, `submissions/process-sop-architect`, MIT. The discipline of settling the trigger, start, end and exclusions before any step is written; one owner per step; both paths for every decision; facts kept apart from assumptions; missing facts marked and listed as open questions, asked one at a time; limits, approvals and policy requirements never invented; a long procedure split into parts (the source's ceiling is 25 steps, this template's is 20). No text is reused. Its licence notice at that commit is in [notices/microsoft-cat-agent-skills-LICENSE.txt](notices/microsoft-cat-agent-skills-LICENSE.txt).
- **[alirezarezvani/claude-skills](https://github.com/alirezarezvani/claude-skills)** at commit `19392f7a08264ed00486a251f5b2098321771f94`, `business-operations/skills/knowledge-ops`, MIT. The checks that keep a procedure usable: a named owner and a review date, an observable sign that each step is done, a note on any step that cannot be undone and who is asked, a team never standing in for a named role, and coverage of the common ways the work goes wrong. No text is reused. Its licence notice at that commit is in [notices/alirezarezvani-claude-skills-LICENSE.txt](notices/alirezarezvani-claude-skills-LICENSE.txt).
